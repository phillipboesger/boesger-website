What it does

Today, unified

Apple Reminders and Calendar events combined into one daily timeline — tap to complete, swipe to reschedule, or add a todo from free text.

Journal with on-device AI

A short daily check-in with optional task suggestions derived from your free text, powered entirely by Apple's on-device FoundationModels framework.

Quick, rich-text Notes

Bold, italic, underline, headings and lists, with a settable title that falls back to the first line.

Apple stays the source of truth

Reminders and Calendar events sync only through your own iCloud account — Daily Board never keeps a second, parallel copy.

No account, no tracking

No sign-up, no analytics, no advertising identifiers, no third-party SDKs. Journal entries and notes live only in the app's local store.

German & English

Fully localized, with English as the fallback for any device language that isn't German.

Privacy, in full

Daily Board does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data to my or any third party's servers. This is the complete privacy policy, not a summary.

Reminders & Calendar

Displayed and edited in the daily board, stored on your device and synced only via your own iCloud account (Apple's technology, not mine). No second copy is ever kept.

Journal & Notes

Stored exclusively on-device (SwiftData). Deleted when you remove an entry, or when you uninstall the app.

On-device AI

Optional journal task suggestions run entirely locally via Apple's FoundationModels framework — no text is ever sent to a server, mine, Apple's, or anyone else's.

Backup

An optional local JSON backup (weekly or manual) lands in the app's own Documents folder, visible via the Files app — you decide if and where to move it. The app never uploads it automatically.

No sharing with third parties

No advertising, no analytics or tracking services, no data sharing, no in-app purchases, no cloud backend run by me.
