This policy covers boesger.com and the project sites polarion-mcp.boesger.com, polarion.code.editor.boesger.com and polarion-docker.boesger.com (see the imprint). The blog and photography site have their own additional policies.

Hosting and security

The pages are static files served by GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., USA). boesger.com, polarion-mcp.boesger.com and polarion-docker.boesger.com also run through Cloudflare (Cloudflare, Inc., USA) as a content delivery network and to block attacks. To deliver a page, these providers process your IP address, date and time, the requested URL, HTTP status, amount of data transferred and technical browser information. This is needed to serve the pages, investigate errors and fend off abuse.

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR. My legitimate interest is a secure, reliably available website. Technical logs are kept only as long as needed for operation, troubleshooting or investigating a security incident; how long the providers store them depends on their service. More information: GitHub privacy statement, Cloudflare privacy policy.

No tracking, no analytics cookies

This site sets no analytics or advertising cookies and does not use localStorage or sessionStorage to recognise you. Fonts, images and scripts are served from this domain. A Content Security Policy blocks analytics scripts and automatic connections to third-party services. Links to GitHub, social networks, the booking calendar or AI assistants only contact those services when you click them.

That is why there is no cookie banner: there is nothing optional to consent to. If Cloudflare sets a security cookie to stop concrete abuse, it serves only the secure delivery of the page you requested (Section 25(2) No. 2 TDDDG) and is not used for tracking.

Contacting me by email or form

When you get in touch, I process your email address, the name you give (optional) and your message in order to reply. The form is handled by my self-hosted workflow server at n8n.boesger.com, behind Cloudflare, and delivered to my inbox through my Gmail account (Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland). The form workflow does not store message contents. To limit abuse, your IP address is kept for at most 30 minutes.

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR if your request concerns a contract, otherwise Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR, my legitimate interest being to answer you and keep spam out. Contacting me does not sign you up for anything. I delete messages once the request is closed, unless statutory retention periods or open claims require me to keep them. Providing your details is voluntary, but without a working email address and a message I cannot reply.

Recipients and transfers outside the EU

The providers for hosting, security and email receive only the data their task needs. GitHub, Cloudflare and Google may process data in the USA or other third countries. Transfers rely on the safeguards that apply to each service, such as an adequacy decision under Art. 45 GDPR or appropriate safeguards under Art. 46 GDPR. Details are in the providers' privacy policies (e.g. Google privacy policy) and available from me on request.

Your rights

Under the conditions set by law you have the right of access (Art. 15 GDPR), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction of processing (Art. 18) and data portability (Art. 20). You can object to processing based on legitimate interests on grounds relating to your particular situation (Art. 21), and withdraw any consent with effect for the future (Art. 7(3)).

A short message to the address above is enough to exercise these rights. You can also lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority, for example the Saxon Data Protection and Transparency Commissioner. There is no automated decision-making or profiling under Art. 22 GDPR.

Private Google OAuth application

Separately from this website, I run a self-hosted automation system ("Second Brain") for strictly personal, non-commercial use. It connects through Google OAuth to my own Google account only:

  • Google Calendar: read and create events
  • Google Drive: read files
  • Gmail: read messages, create drafts

The only purpose is my own organisation and automation, such as a calendar overview, knowledge management and drafting my emails. It is not offered to anyone else, and no other users have access. Google API data is stored on infrastructure I operate. When I use a connected AI assistant, selected content can be processed by that assistant's provider, such as OpenAI or Anthropic, to answer my request. Use of Google API data follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Access can be revoked at any time in the Google account settings at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
