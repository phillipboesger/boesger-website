BRAND TOKENS 
/* Bösger Digital – Brand Tokens */
:root {
  /* ── Backgrounds ── */
  --bg-deep:      #000D1C;   /* Page background */
  --bg-base:      #001222;   /* Sections */
  --bg-surface:   #062237;   /* Cards, panels */
  --bg-elevated:  #124061;   /* Hover, borders */

  /* ── Accent – Cyan ── */
  --accent:       #01BFFB;   /* Primary brand cyan */
  --accent-light: #6BDFF1;   /* Glow, highlights */
  --accent-deep:  #058FC1;   /* Active, gradient */
  --accent-muted: #336D91;   /* Disabled, subtle */
  --accent-tint:  rgba(1,191,251,0.12);

  /* ── Typography ── */
  --text-primary: #FFFFFF;   /* Headings */
  --text-body:    #C0F6FC;   /* Body, subheadings */
  --text-muted:   #336D91;   /* Placeholder, meta */
}

Bösger Digital – Complete Brand Color Reference

Background Colors

  • Deep Navy – #000D1C – rgb(0,13,28) – CSS: --bg-deep – Use: Page background, deepest level
  • Dark Navy – #001222 – rgb(0,18,34) – CSS: --bg-base – Use: Sections and areas
  • Surface Navy – #062237 – rgb(6,34,55) – CSS: --bg-surface – Use: Cards and panels
  • Elevated – #124061 – rgb(18,64,97) – CSS: --bg-elevated – Use: Hover states, borders, dividers

Accent Colors (Brand Cyan)

  • Cyan Primary – #01BFFB – rgb(1,191,251) – CSS: --accent – Use: Logo, CTAs, links, icons
  • Cyan Light – #6BDFF1 – rgb(107,223,241) – CSS: --accent-light – Use: Hover, glow, highlights
  • Cyan Deep – #058FC1 – rgb(5,143,193) – CSS: --accent-deep – Use: Active states, gradient end
  • Cyan Muted – #336D91 – rgb(51,109,145) – CSS: --accent-muted – Use: Disabled, subtle UI
  • Cyan Tint – rgba(1,191,251,0.12) – CSS: --accent-tint – Use: Tinted backgrounds and badges

Typography Colors

  • White – #FFFFFF – rgb(255,255,255) – CSS: --text-primary – Use: Headings H1–H3
  • Ice Blue – #C0F6FC – rgb(192,246,252) – CSS: --text-body – Use: Body text, subheadings
  • Muted Blue – #336D91 – rgb(51,109,145) – CSS: --text-muted – Use: Placeholders, labels, meta
