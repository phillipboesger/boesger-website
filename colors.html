  /* ── Backgrounds ── */

  --bg-surface:   #1F6099;   /* Cards, panels */
  --bg-elevated:  #3A82C4;   /* Hover, borders */

  /* ── Accent – Cyan ── */
  --accent:       #B0EFFF;   /* CTAs, links, icons */
  --accent-light: #DCF8FF;   /* Glow, highlights */

  /* ── Warm counterpoint ── */
  --warm:         #FFC766;   /* Availability, highlights */
  --on-warm:      #0D3A68;   /* Text on cyan and amber */

  /* ── Typography ── */
  --text-primary: #FFFFFF;   /* Headings */
  --text-body:    #E6F2FB;   /* Body, subheadings */
  --text-muted:   #CFE3F5;   /* Labels, meta */
}

Bösger Digital – Complete Brand Color Reference

Background Colors

  • Deep Blue – #18508A – rgb(24, 80, 138) – CSS: --bg-deep – Use: Page background, deepest level
  • Base Blue – #1C5994 – rgb(28, 89, 148) – CSS: --bg-base – Use: Sections and areas
  • Surface Blue – #1F6099 – rgb(31, 96, 153) – CSS: --bg-surface – Use: Cards and panels
  • Elevated Blue – #3A82C4 – rgb(58, 130, 196) – CSS: --bg-elevated – Use: Hover states, borders, dividers

Accent Colors (Cyan)

  • Ice Cyan – #B0EFFF – rgb(176, 239, 255) – CSS: --accent – Use: CTAs, links, icons, eyebrow labels
  • Cyan Light – #DCF8FF – rgb(220, 248, 255) – CSS: --accent-light – Use: Hover, glow, highlights
  • Cyan Deep – #5FCAF7 – rgb(95, 202, 247) – CSS: --accent-deep – Use: Active states, chips, gradient end
  • Cyan Muted – #7FB4E0 – rgb(127, 180, 224) – CSS: --accent-muted – Use: Disabled, subtle UI
  • Cyan Tint – rgba(176,239,255,0.14) – CSS: --accent-tint – Use: Tinted backgrounds and badges

Warm Counterpoint (Amber)

  • Amber – #FFC766 – rgb(255, 199, 102) – CSS: --warm – Use: Availability, prices, key highlights (the one warm counterpoint)
  • Ink on Light – #0D3A68 – rgb(13, 58, 104) – CSS: --text-on-accent – Use: Text on cyan buttons and amber badges
  • Logo Cyan – #01BFFB – rgb(1, 191, 251) – Use: Original logo cyan, used in the logo and icon files only

Typography Colors

  • White – #FFFFFF – rgb(255, 255, 255) – CSS: --text-primary – Use: Headings
  • Ice Blue – #E6F2FB – rgb(230, 242, 251) – CSS: --text-body – Use: Body text, subheadings
  • Soft Blue – #CFE3F5 – rgb(207, 227, 245) – CSS: --text-muted – Use: Labels, meta text (4.5:1 or better on every surface)
